# Five-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:30 — Problem

Payment teams need to act quickly when activity looks suspicious, but pure rules create noisy queues and black-box scores do not explain decisions.

## 0:30–1:15 — Overview dashboard

Show payment volume, risk distribution, review backlog, blocked exposure, and risk trends. Emphasize that the demo uses synthetic data only.

## 1:15–2:30 — Suspicious transaction

Open a transaction flagged for a new device, unusual location, repeated attempts, and an amount well above the customer baseline. Show the composite score and its ML, rules, and anomaly components.

## 2:30–3:30 — Explainability

Show the human-readable factors and triggered policies. Explain that the system keeps model/version metadata and evidence for auditability.

## 3:30–4:30 — AI investigation

Run the investigation agent. It retrieves only approved transaction, customer-history, device, and policy evidence, then returns a structured recommendation: Allow, Manual Review, or Block—with confidence and uncertainty.

## 4:30–5:00 — Human control and impact

Record an analyst decision or override. Close with the feedback loop: decisions improve evaluation and later model-training workflows, while policy and humans stay in control.
